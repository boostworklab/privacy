1. Information We Process & Operational Architecture

CoordiPro adheres to strict data minimization and privacy-by-design principles. The application is an in-process plugin for Autodesk® AutoCAD® and operates under the following conditions:

  • Zero CAD / Drawing Data Transmission: All coordinate evaluations, frame offset conversions (e.g., FR54+250), centerline calculations, deck height referencing, and Multileader (MLeader) entity placements execute strictly on your local computer. Active project links are saved directly into the active drawing database (.dwg) using standard AutoCAD Named Objects Dictionaries (DBDictionary / Xrecord). No drawing geometry, coordinates, or proprietary project structures are ever transmitted externally.
  • Payment & Order Processing (Lemon Squeezy): All orders, checkout transactions, invoicing, and tax/VAT calculations are securely managed by our merchant of record, Lemon Squeezy. BoostWorkLab never collects, sees, or stores your credit card, debit card, or banking credentials.
  • License Activation & Seat Validation: When activating or verifying a license key, the application communicates securely over HTTPS (TLS 1.2/1.3) with our serverless validation API powered by Lemon Squeezy. It transmits only your license key and an anonymized hardware identifier hash (a cryptographically salted SHA-384 digest generated locally) to enforce single-user or multi-seat license activation limits.
  • Offline Grace Period & Data Security: Cached license tokens and validation states are encrypted on your workstation using the Windows Data Protection API (DPAPI) with current-user scope, allowing seamless offline CAD workflows for up to 7 days without continuous internet connectivity.
  • Local User & Project Presets: Project parameters (including frame intervals, deck elevations, longitudinal offsets, text styles, arrowheads, and landing gaps) are stored locally on your workstation at %APPDATA%\CoordiPro\coordipro_projects.json. This data is utilized solely by your local AutoCAD sessions and is never uploaded.
  • User-Initiated Feedback & Diagnostics: If you choose to submit feedback or bug reports via the Configuration dialog (CPCONFIGURATIONS), only the text you provide along with basic diagnostic details (AutoCAD version, OS version, CoordiPro version, and a masked license key) are transmitted. This action is 100% voluntary and user-initiated.
  • Zero Tracking SDKs: CoordiPro contains no background telemetry beacons, marketing trackers, advertising SDKs, or keystroke loggers.

2. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with third-party advertising networks, data brokers, or analytics companies. We only interface with trusted operational service providers necessary for billing, licensing, and distribution:

  • Lemon Squeezy: Merchant of record handling order processing, PCI-DSS compliant payment processing, and license seat management with enterprise-grade encryption. (Lemon Squeezy Privacy Policy)
  • Autodesk® App Store: If downloaded via the Autodesk App Store, distribution and account access are governed by Autodesk's Terms of Service and Privacy Statement.
  • GitHub: Hosts public documentation, version update manifests (version.json), setup installers, and issue trackers over secure HTTPS.

3. Data Retention & Deletion

  • Licensing Records: License activation records and anonymized hardware associations are retained in Lemon Squeezy solely for the duration of your active license or as required for ongoing technical support and tax compliance.
  • Local Storage Removal: You can purge all local plugin settings and cached databases at any time by deleting the folders %APPDATA%\CoordiPro and %LOCALAPPDATA%\BoostWorkLab\CoordiPro.
  • Drawing Metadata: Project reference dictionaries stored inside your CAD files remain entirely under your control and can be purged from any .dwg drawing at any time.
  • Complete Uninstallation: Uninstalling CoordiPro via the Windows Control Panel / Add-Remove Programs cleanly removes the application binaries, autoloader bundle, and CUIX ribbons from your computer.

4. User Rights & Legal Compliance (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

CoordiPro complies fully with international privacy regulations, including the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA).

Under these regulations, you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any licensing records associated with your email address. Because we do not collect or store your CAD drawings, geometries, or engineering telemetry, you maintain 100% sovereignty and exclusive ownership over your design data.
