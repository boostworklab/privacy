Publisher Privacy Policy

Application: CoordiPro for Autodesk® AutoCAD®  |  Effective Date: August 2026

Summary: CoordiPro operates locally on your workstation. We do not collect, transmit, view, or store your CAD drawings, geometries, or design files (.dwg/.dxf). We never sell your data or share it with third-party advertising or analytics networks.

1. Information We Collect & How It Is Used

We practice data minimization and collect only the minimum information necessary to provide and license the application:

  • No CAD / Drawing Data: CoordiPro operates 100% locally within Autodesk® AutoCAD®. No drawing geometry, coordinates, layer data, project metadata, or CAD files (.dwg, .dxf) are collected, monitored, or transmitted to external servers.
  • License Activation & Validation: When activating a paid license, the application transmits the license key and an anonymized hardware identifier (one-way machine hash) over secure HTTPS/TLS to our licensing provider (Lemon Squeezy) strictly to enforce per-seat activation limits.
  • Software Updates: The application may periodically query a public repository manifest over HTTPS to check for new versions. No telemetry, machine profiles, or personal data are collected during update checks.
  • User Preferences: Custom configuration settings (e.g., coordinate formatting, prefix rules, UI settings) are stored locally on your workstation and are never transmitted externally.

2. Third-Party Data Sharing & Protection

We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize user data with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

Third-party service providers involved with our service delivery provide data protection standards equal to or greater than those stated in this Privacy Policy:

  • Lemon Squeezy (Merchant of Record / Licensing): Processes orders, billing, and cryptographic license validation under strict GDPR/CCPA data compliance standards.
  • GitHub: Hosts public application setup files and update manifests over HTTPS.

3. Data Retention & Deletion Policy

  • Retention: License activation records and anonymized hardware hashes are retained only for the duration of the active software license/subscription to maintain product support.
  • Deletion: Upon license termination, expiration, or upon customer request, associated licensing records and hardware hashes are permanently purged within 30 days.
  • Local Storage: All locally stored configurations and caches are permanently removed when uninstalling the application from your workstation.

4. User Consent, Revocation & Data Deletion Requests

Users have the right to revoke consent and request deletion of their licensing information at any time under GDPR/CCPA regulations:

  • To request the deletion of your customer records or license history, email our support team at support@boostworklab.com.
  • Requests are verified and completed within 30 days.
  • You may cease all local data processing immediately by uninstalling the application.
