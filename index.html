1. Information We Process & Operational Architecture

CoordiPro adheres to strict data minimization and privacy-by-design principles. The application is an in-process plugin for Autodesk AutoCAD and operates under the following conditions:

  • Zero CAD Telemetry: All coordinate evaluations, frame offset conversions (e.g., FR54+250), centerline calculations, deck height referencing, and Multileader (MLeader) entity placements execute strictly on your local computer. Active project links are saved directly into the active drawing database (.dwg) using standard AutoCAD Named Objects Dictionaries (DBDictionary / Xrecord). No drawing geometry, coordinates, or proprietary project structures are ever transmitted externally.
  • Payment & Order Processing (Lemon Squeezy): All orders, checkout transactions, invoicing, and tax/VAT calculations are securely managed by our merchant of record, Lemon Squeezy. BoostWorkLab never collects, sees, or stores your credit card, debit card, or banking credentials.
  • License Activation & Seat Validation: When activating or verifying a license key, the application communicates securely over HTTPS (TLS 1.2/1.3) with our serverless validation API powered by Lemon Squeezy. It transmits only your license key and an anonymized hardware identifier hash. This hash is a cryptographically salted SHA-384 digest generated locally on your machine, and it is used solely to enforce single-user or multi-seat license activation limits.
  • Offline Grace Period & Data Security: Cached license tokens and validation states are encrypted on your workstation using the Windows Data Protection API (DPAPI) with current-user scope, allowing seamless offline CAD workflows for up to 3 days without continuous internet connectivity.
  • Local User & Project Presets: Project parameters (including frame intervals, deck elevations, longitudinal offsets, text styles, arrowheads, and landing gaps) are stored locally on your workstation at %APPDATA%\CoordiPro\coordipro_projects.json. This data is used solely by your local AutoCAD sessions and is never uploaded.
  • Trial Period Tracking: During the 30-day evaluation period, CoordiPro stores an encrypted trial start/last-used timestamp pair, cryptographically signed to detect tampering. This timestamp pair is written redundantly to several local locations on your workstation (Windows Registry and cache files) so that clearing any single location does not disrupt normal trial operation. This data never leaves your computer and contains no drawing or personal information beyond the timestamps themselves.
  • User-Initiated Feedback & Diagnostics: If you choose to submit feedback or bug reports via the Configuration dialog (CPCONFIGURATIONS), the text you provide is transmitted along with basic diagnostic details: AutoCAD version, OS version, CoordiPro version, license validity status, a masked license key, and your anonymized instance ID. If you optionally provide a reply-to email address, that is transmitted as well. This action is entirely voluntary and user-initiated.
  • Zero Tracking SDKs: CoordiPro contains no background telemetry beacons, marketing trackers, advertising SDKs, or keystroke loggers.

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with third-party advertising networks, data brokers, or analytics companies. We only interface with trusted operational service providers necessary for billing, licensing, support, and distribution:

  • Lemon Squeezy: Merchant of record handling order processing, PCI-DSS compliant payment processing, and license seat management. (Lemon Squeezy Privacy Policy)

  • Autodesk App Store: If downloaded via the Autodesk App Store, distribution and account access are governed by Autodesk's Terms of Service and Privacy Statement. (Autodesk Privacy Statement)
  • GitHub: Hosts our public documentation pages (including this policy and the EULA) and the CoordiPro setup installer download, over secure HTTPS. (GitHub Privacy Statement)

Some of these providers are located in, or process data in, the United States. Where required by applicable law, such transfers rely on appropriate safeguards, including Standard Contractual Clauses, as implemented by each provider.

3. Data Retention & Deletion

  • Licensing Records: License activation records and anonymized hardware associations are retained by Lemon Squeezy for the duration of your active license, or as required for technical support and tax compliance.
  • Local Storage Removal: You can remove locally stored project presets and error logs at any time by deleting the folder %APPDATA%\CoordiPro.
  • License & Trial Data Erasure: Your encrypted license and trial-tracking data are additionally stored in several protected locations on your workstation, including the Windows Registry, so that this data remains available for normal license and trial operation even if one storage location is cleared. Uninstalling CoordiPro does not remove this data automatically. To request full erasure of this data, first deactivate your license from within the application, then contact support@boostworklab.com; we will verify your request and provide guided removal instructions.
  • Drawing Metadata: Project reference dictionaries stored inside your CAD files remain entirely under your control and can be removed from any .dwg drawing at any time.
  • Complete Uninstallation: Uninstalling CoordiPro via Windows Settings / Add-or-Remove Programs removes the application binaries, autoloader bundle, and CUIX ribbons from your computer. It does not remove the license/trial data or local files described above, or files you have created in your drawings.
